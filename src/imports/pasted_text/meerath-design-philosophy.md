Project Overview & Design Philosophy
Create a sophisticated, minimal, and high-end UI/UX design for a 22-year-old legendary Pakistani restaurant: "Meerath Famous Kabab Paratha" located in North Nazimabad, Karachi. The design must project an air of sleek, contemporary luxury, balancing traditional rich desi culinary roots with clean, minimal digital architecture. This is a crucial opportunity to establish a dominant online presence that stands out, not as a dated concept, but as a timeless culinary authority.

Visual System & Color Theory (60-30-10 Rule)
Dominant Color (60%): Deep Charcoal Black to establish a dramatic, elegant, and modern background that exudes luxury and focuses the user's attention.

Secondary Color (30%): Premium White for high readability in body text, descriptions, and structural borders, providing a crisp contrast.

Accent Color (10%): Vibrant Ochre Orange (extracted from the "MEERATH" banner in 1767179871-MEERATH-LOGO.webp) used with precision for high-conversion CTAs, active states, product highlights, and decorative elements.

Brand Asset: Incorporate the logo file 1767179871-MEERATH-LOGO.webp elegantly in the header navigation and relevant branding touchpoints, contrasting sharply with the charcoal background.

Typography Hierarchy (Golden Ratio):

Headings: Bold, clean, geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Clash Display or Syne) for a authoritative, premium impact.

Body/Subtext: Lighter, highly readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) for a clean, minimal look. Maintain perfect golden ratio contrast scaling for font sizes.
